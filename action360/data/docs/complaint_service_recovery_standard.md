# Complaint Handling & Service Recovery Standard
DOC_TYPE: SERVICING_POLICY | INDUSTRY: ALL | PRODUCT: ALL | VERSION: 2026.1

## Service level agreements
Fee reversals must be completed within 7 working days. Any complaint open for more than 14 days, or raised three or more times, must be escalated to a senior relationship manager or grievance officer.

## Service recovery action
For high-value customers (PREMIER or top value decile) with an escalated or repeated complaint, the relationship manager must make a personal call within 24 hours, confirm the resolution timeline in writing, and may apply a goodwill gesture such as a fee waiver (OFF_FEE_WAIVER) where the customer holds a credit card and has no missed payments.

## Escalation
Complaints flagged ESCALATED must be tracked to closure by the grievance desk. The customer must receive a named point of contact.

## Root-cause tagging
Each complaint is tagged with a category: FEES_AND_CHARGES, DIGITAL_SERVICE, STAFF_BEHAVIOUR, CLAIMS_PROCESSING, COLLECTIONS_PRACTICE, PRODUCT_INFORMATION.
