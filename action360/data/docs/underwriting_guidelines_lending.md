# Underwriting Guidelines - Retail Lending
DOC_TYPE: UNDERWRITING_GUIDELINE | INDUSTRY: LENDING | PRODUCT: HL,PL,AL | VERSION: 2026.1

## Rate reset review
At the end of a fixed-rate period, the loan moves to the floating benchmark plus spread. Customers may request a repricing review; pricing desk approval is required for any reduction greater than 25 basis points.

## Required documents
Rate reset reviews and top-ups require income proof (last 3 salary slips or ITR) dated within 90 days. If documents are pending, the relationship manager should send a missing-document request before any approval.

## Credit policy
Minimum credit score of 700 for new unsecured lending; 650 for secured. Debt-to-income must not exceed 50% after any top-up.

## Foreclosure and balance transfer
On a foreclosure enquiry, the retention desk must be notified within 1 business day. Foreclosure statements must be issued within 3 business days as per regulatory guidance.
