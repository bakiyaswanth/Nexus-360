# Motor & Home Insurance Renewal Guidelines
DOC_TYPE: RETENTION_OFFER | INDUSTRY: INSURANCE | PRODUCT: MOTOR,HOME_INS | VERSION: 2026.1

## Renewal outreach window
Renewal reminders begin 45 days before expiry. Customers with an open claim must be contacted by the claims team BEFORE any renewal pricing conversation.

## Loyalty renewal discount (OFF_LOYALTY_RENEWAL)
A 10% loyalty discount may be offered when the policy renews within 60 days, the customer has made at most 1 claim in the last 12 months, there are no missed premiums in 6 months, and tenure is at least 36 months. Customers with 2 or more claims in 12 months are NOT eligible; their renewal premium reflects claims experience as filed with the regulator.

## No-claim bonus protector (OFF_NCB_PROTECT)
Motor policyholders with tenure of 12 months or more may add an NCB protector at renewal.

## Open claims
A delayed claim (beyond 30 days without surveyor report) must be escalated to the claims manager and the customer given a settlement timeline. Resolving the claim is the most effective retention lever for these customers.
