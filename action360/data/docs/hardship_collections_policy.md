# Financial Hardship & Collections Policy
DOC_TYPE: SERVICING_POLICY | INDUSTRY: LENDING | PRODUCT: HL,PL,AL,CC | VERSION: 2026.2

## Principle
Customers experiencing genuine financial hardship (job loss, medical emergency, business disruption) must be offered a hardship conversation before any further collection escalation. The goal is to keep the customer current and protect their credit record, not to sell.

## Hardship assistance plan (OFF_HARDSHIP_PLAN)
Eligible when the customer has at least one missed or partial instalment in the last 6 months OR maximum days past due of 15 or more in the last 90 days, and the customer's credit score is at least 550. Options include EMI restructuring with tenure extension, a payment holiday of up to 3 months on unsecured loans, or a step-up EMI. Late fees accrued during the hardship period may be waived on approval.

## What not to offer
Customers with missed instalments or in hardship must NOT be offered cross-sell, product upgrades, top-up loans or retention cashback. These increase indebtedness and are inconsistent with responsible lending.

## Collections contact limits
No more than one collection call per day and no calls before 08:00 or after 19:00. If a customer has requested a hardship review, collection calls must pause until the review is complete.

## Communication tone
Use empathetic, non-judgemental language. Acknowledge the customer's history of on-time payments where applicable. Prefer the customer's preferred channel.
