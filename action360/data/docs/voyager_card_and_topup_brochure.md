# Voyager Premium Travel Credit Card - Product Brochure
DOC_TYPE: PRODUCT_BROCHURE | INDUSTRY: LENDING | PRODUCT: CC | VERSION: 2026.1

## Benefits
Unlimited domestic and 8 international lounge visits per year, 5x reward points on travel and foreign currency spend, 1.5% forex markup, complimentary travel insurance up to INR 50 lakh.

## Upgrade eligibility (OFF_CARD_UPGRADE)
Existing credit card holders qualify for an instant upgrade when their credit score is at least 750, they have had no missed or late payments in the last 6 months, and their card spend over the last 3 months is at least 10% higher than the previous 3 months. Annual fee INR 4,999, waived on annual spend above INR 5 lakh.

## Pre-approved top-up loan (OFF_TOPUP_LOAN)
Customers holding a home or personal loan with a credit score of at least 740, tenure of at least 12 months and no missed instalments in 6 months may be offered a pre-approved top-up of up to 20% of the original principal at the existing rate.

## Responsible selling
Upgrades and top-ups must not be offered to customers with open complaints or payment difficulty.
