# Customer Communication Guidelines
DOC_TYPE: COMMUNICATION_GUIDELINE | INDUSTRY: ALL | PRODUCT: ALL | VERSION: 2026.1

## Tone
Be warm, specific and concise. Acknowledge the customer's relationship and any problem they have experienced before making any proposal. Never use pressure language such as "limited time only" for hardship or complaint cases.

## Channel
Use the customer's preferred channel. For escalated complaints and high-value customers, a personal phone call is preferred over email.

## Content rules
Never include full account numbers, balances or personal identifiers in outbound messages. Do not reference internal risk scores. Do not name competitors. Only reference offers the customer is eligible for.

## Structure of an outreach message
1. Personal acknowledgement. 2. What we are doing for them. 3. A clear next step with a named contact or time. 4. A short sign-off.
