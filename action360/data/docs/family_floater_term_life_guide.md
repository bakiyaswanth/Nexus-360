# Family Floater Health & Term Life - Product Guide
DOC_TYPE: PRODUCT_BROCHURE | INDUSTRY: INSURANCE | PRODUCT: HEALTH,FAMILY_FLOATER,TERM_LIFE | VERSION: 2026.2

## Family floater (OFF_FAMILY_FLOATER)
A single sum insured shared by self, spouse and up to 3 children. Newborns are covered from day 91, or from day 1 if the mother is covered under the same floater. Existing individual health policyholders aged 55 or below can migrate to a family floater with continuity of waiting-period credit for the existing insured member.

## Term life cover (OFF_TERM_LIFE)
Pure protection cover of 10-20x annual income. Eligible for customers aged 60 or below with no existing term life policy with us. Recommended after life events such as marriage, the birth of a child or a new home loan.

## Coverage review
A coverage review should be offered when a customer reports a life event (marriage, newborn, home purchase) or holds only one protection product. Advisors should assess health, life and home protection needs together.

## Underwriting notes
Term cover above INR 1 crore requires income proof and a medical examination. Family floater migration does not require fresh medicals for members under 45.
