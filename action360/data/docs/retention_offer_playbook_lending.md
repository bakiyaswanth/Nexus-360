# Retention Offer Playbook (Lending)
DOC_TYPE: RETENTION_OFFER | INDUSTRY: LENDING | PRODUCT: HL,PL,CC | VERSION: 2026.3

## Purpose
This playbook defines when relationship managers may present a retention offer to a lending customer who shows attrition signals such as a foreclosure enquiry, a balance-transfer request, or a competitor mention on a call.

## Home loan rate match (OFF_RATE_MATCH)
A rate match of up to 60 basis points below the customer's current rate, floored at 8.35%, may be offered for home loans or personal loans when ALL of the following hold: customer tenure is at least 24 months, there have been no missed instalments in the last 6 months, the maximum days past due in the last 90 days is 5 or less, the customer segment is AFFLUENT or PREMIER, and the churn risk score is at least 0.40. The rate match is valid for 30 days from the offer date.

## Service before price
If a customer has an open, unresolved complaint, the complaint must be resolved or formally escalated BEFORE a pricing retention offer is presented. Leading with a discount while a grievance is unresolved is perceived as dismissive and historically converts at less than half the rate of a service-recovery-first approach. The retention offer may be mentioned as a follow-up once the service issue is acknowledged.

## Generic retention cashback (OFF_RETENTION_CASHBACK)
A one-time cashback of INR 5,000 may be offered to any customer with churn risk of at least 0.50, zero missed instalments in 6 months, and maximum days past due of 30 or less. It must never be offered to customers in active collections or with a hardship request.

## Prohibited practices
Do not present a retention offer to a customer who is not eligible under the hard rules above. Do not promise a rate that has not been approved by the pricing desk. Do not use competitor names in written communication.
